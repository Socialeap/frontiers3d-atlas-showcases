"There Is a Certain Slant of Light" - Pratt Manhattan Gallery

New York, NY

A fine-art exhibition at Pratt Institute's Manhattan gallery on West 14th Street, curated by Seph Rodney, exploring the poetic use of light across painting, photography, and multimedia. Featuring a diverse group of Pratt MFA alumni, it ran June through September 2023 in the school's Chelsea-adjacent exhibition space.
